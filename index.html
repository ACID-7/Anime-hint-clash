      How this version works

      • Each character has a bigger clue bank than the 10 hints you actually see.
      • The engine mixes vague, medium, and direct clues so repeats feel different.
      • Recently used characters and recently shown hints are pushed out of rotation first.
      • Aliases work too, so short names like Gojo or Luffy can still count.
      • Everything is static, so it can be hosted straight on Netlify.
