How to Play

  • Each round hides one anime character. Up to 10 hints are revealed.
  • You get one guess per hint. Correct = points. Wrong = reveal another hint.
  • Earlier guesses score more. Aliases (e.g. Gojo, Luffy) count.
  • Choose an anime category to guess only from that series.
